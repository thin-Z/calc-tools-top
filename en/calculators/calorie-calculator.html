Calorie Calculator Guide

The Calorie Calculator is a completely free online tool that estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula from your gender, age, height, weight and activity level. It then multiplies BMR by an activity factor to get your daily maintenance calories (TDEE), plus recommended ranges for weight loss and muscle gain.

What are BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the minimum energy your body needs at rest. TDEE adds daily activity and exercise to that baseline. To maintain weight, eat around TDEE; to lose weight, eat slightly below it; to build muscle, eat slightly above it.

How to choose your activity level

Sedentary (desk job, little exercise) → ×1.2; exercise 1-3 days/week → ×1.375; 3-5 days/week → ×1.55; 6-7 days/week → ×1.725; physical labor or twice-daily training → ×1.9.
