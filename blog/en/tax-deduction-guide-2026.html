2026 Tax Deduction Guide: All Seven Allowances Explained

China's seven special additional deductions are the easiest money a salaried employee can claim and the easiest money to lose. In 2026 the seven allowances keep the raised limits, and a household with one child, a first-home mortgage and dependent parents can remove CNY 54,000 from taxable income every year. At a marginal rate of 10% to 25%, that is CNY 5,400 to 13,500 back annually.

The money does not arrive automatically. You have to confirm the information, decide which spouse claims each item, and know which allowances stack and which are mutually exclusive. This guide uses the 2026 published limits to lay out all seven items, the tax arithmetic behind them, the allocation decision within a marriage, and a claim checklist you can copy.

Housing rent splits by city tier: CNY 1,500/month for municipalities, provincial capitals and cities with separate state planning; CNY 1,100/month where the urban population exceeds one million; CNY 800/month below that.

Three rules catch people out. Housing loan interest and housing rent are mutually exclusive. Elderly care is calculated per dependant, not per elderly person, so supporting both parents does not double the allowance. And major medical expenses can only be claimed at the annual settlement, never through monthly withholding.

4. How a Couple Should Split the Allowances

Children's education, infant care and housing loan interest all involve a "who claims it" decision. The rules: children's education and infant care can be split 50/50 or claimed entirely by one parent, while housing loan interest goes entirely to one spouse. The allocation principle is single: put the deduction where the marginal rate is higher.

AllocationArithmeticCombined annual saving
Split 50/5012,000 × 20% + 12,000 × 10%CNY 3,600
All to the 20% spouse24,000 × 20%CNY 4,800
Difference—CNY 1,200 per year

If both spouses sit in the same bracket, the split makes no difference. When the brackets differ, concentrating the allowance saves real money. The same logic applies to mortgage interest: CNY 1,000 a month is worth CNY 2,400 a year in the 20% bracket and only CNY 1,200 in the 10% bracket.

5. What Else Reduces Taxable Income

Taxable income is computed in a fixed order: comprehensive income, minus the basic deduction, minus social insurance and housing fund contributions, minus other allowances, then minus the seven special deductions.

Stacked together, someone earning CNY 180,000 a year can push the taxable base down to just over CNY 40,000. That layered reduction — not the CNY 5,000 threshold alone — is what actually determines take-home pay.

6. Three Worked Examples

Example 1: a two-earner household on CNY 180,000. Social insurance and housing fund contributions come to CNY 24,000 a year, and the qualifying allowances are children's education CNY 24,000, housing loan interest CNY 12,000 and elderly care at the shared CNY 1,500/month rate (CNY 18,000) — CNY 54,000 in total. Without the special deductions the taxable base is 180,000 − 60,000 − 24,000 = 96,000, giving tax of 9,600 − 2,520 = CNY 7,080. With them the base falls to 42,000 and tax to 4,200 − 2,520 = CNY 1,680. The household keeps CNY 5,400 more, exactly 54,000 multiplied by the 10% marginal rate.

Example 2: CNY 300,000 income with the same deductions. Contributions are CNY 36,000. Without the allowances the base is 204,000 and tax 204,000 × 20% − 16,920 = CNY 23,880; with them the base drops to 150,000 and tax to 150,000 × 20% − 16,920 = CNY 13,080. The saving is CNY 10,800, double Example 1, because the marginal rate rose from 10% to 20%.

Example 3: CNY 480,000 income. Contributions are CNY 54,000. Without the allowances the base is 366,000 and tax 366,000 × 25% − 31,920 = CNY 59,580; with them the base falls to 312,000 and tax to 312,000 × 25% − 31,920 = CNY 46,080. The saving is CNY 13,500, matching the 25% marginal rate.

All three examples point the same way: the same deduction is worth more at a higher income. 7. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I claim both mortgage interest and rent?

No. They are mutually exclusive. Mortgage interest is CNY 1,000 a month, while rent reaches CNY 1,500 a month in a provincial capital or municipality, so renting is often worth more if you own no home in your city of work.

Does elderly care multiply with the number of parents?

No. The allowance covers the dependants as a group: CNY 3,000 a month for an only child, or a shared CNY 3,000 split among siblings at no more than CNY 1,500 each. Supporting two parents does not double it.

How should a couple split children's education?

One parent may claim 100%, or both may claim 50% each. Put it with the spouse in the higher bracket — on CNY 24,000 a year the difference between a 20% and a 10% spouse is roughly CNY 1,200 annually.

Does changing jobs mid-year break the deductions?

No. The allowances accumulate on an annual basis. After changing jobs, update the withholding agent in the tax app and the annual settlement reconciles everything at year end.

What if I miss the December confirmation window?

You can add or amend allowance information at any time. Claiming during withholding reduces tax immediately; claiming later means the refund arrives at the annual settlement instead.

Why don't major medical expenses appear in monthly withholding?

Because the allowance is claimed on actual receipts against an annual cap of CNY 80,000, it can only be assessed after the year closes, during the annual settlement.

How are the rent tiers decided?

By city: CNY 1,500 per month for municipalities, provincial capitals and separate-planning cities; CNY 1,100 where the urban population exceeds one million; CNY 800 below that.

Quick tip: run a mid-year allowance check rather than waiting for December. Adjusting a claim or an allocation mid-year changes your withholding immediately, which smooths cash flow far better than discovering an underpayment at filing time.
