About Electricity Calculator

Electricity Calculator is a completely free online tool that helps you Calculate electricity bill and usage. Whether you are a casual user or a professional, you can use it instantly without installing any software.

Whether you want to know your appliance power usage, manage household costs, or estimate energy bills, you can quickly calculate electricity expenses.

How to Use

  1. Enter the appliance wattage in the "Power" field.
  2. Enter daily usage hours and the number of days.
  3. Enter your electricity rate (per kWh).
  4. Click "Calculate" to see daily/monthly usage and cost.
