About Resize & Crop

Resize & Crop is a completely free online tool that helps you Resize and crop images. Whether you are a casual user or a professional, you can use it instantly without installing any software.

It is especially useful for writers, editors, students and web developers who work with text every day and need quick, reliable processing.

How to Use Resize & Crop

  1. Paste or type your text into the input area.
  2. Configure the options to match your exact requirements.
  3. Click the Process button to get the result immediately.
  4. Use the copy button to save the output to your clipboard.

Resize & Crop FAQ

Is Resize & Crop free to use?

Yes, Resize & Crop is completely free. There are no hidden fees, no account registration and no download required.

How accurate are the results?

All calculations are performed locally in your browser using well-established formulas, so the results are accurate and consistent.

Is my data private?

Your privacy is a priority. Everything you enter is processed directly on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Does Resize & Crop work on mobile?

Yes. The page is fully responsive and works smoothly on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Which image formats are supported?

You can resize and crop PNG, JPEG and WebP images, and download the result as PNG or JPEG. Because the Canvas API processes everything locally, there is no file size upload limit and no queue — large photos are handled just as fast as small ones.
