Fuel Cost Calculator: How to Calculate Car Fuel Consumption

A fuel cost calculator turns distance and consumption into a real trip budget. It helps you plan road trips, compare vehicles, and manage commuting costs.

What You Need to Calculate

Enter the distance, your car's fuel consumption (L/100 km or MPG), and the fuel price. The tool converts between units so the result works in any market.

City vs Highway Consumption

Real-world consumption differs from the sticker. City driving with stops burns more fuel per kilometer than steady highway cruising, so use your actual average for accurate budgets.

Comparing Vehicles

When comparing a fuel-efficient car with a hybrid or EV, calculate the cost per 100 km including energy prices—not just the fuel economy numbers.

Trip Budgeting Tips

Add a 10% buffer for detours and traffic, and check current prices at your route's stops. Small habits like steady speeds and proper tire pressure genuinely lower fuel use.

Fuel Prices and Regional Differences

Fuel prices vary significantly between regions and over time due to taxes, supply, and local competition. Use a realistic local price in the calculator rather than a national average for accurate trip budgets.

If your route crosses regions with different prices, calculate each segment separately. The savings from fueling at a cheaper stop can be meaningful on long trips.

How to Calculate Trip Fuel Cost

  1. Enter the trip distance and your car's real-world consumption.
  2. Add the current fuel price at your local station.
  3. Read the total cost and add a buffer for detours and traffic.

Use your own average consumption from recent fill-ups instead of the manufacturer figure for a budget you can rely on.

Quick tip: log your fill-ups for a month to get a real consumption figure. The difference between your actual and the sticker number can change a trip budget by ten percent or more.
