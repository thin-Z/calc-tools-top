About the Workday Calculator

The Workday Calculator is a completely free online tool that counts the real "workable days" between two dates. Whether you are in HR, project management, finance or just an ordinary employee, you can run it instantly in your browser with no software install. Unlike a plain date calculator that only counts calendar difference, this tool strips out weekends and public holidays to give you an "effective working day" count that matches reality.

What exactly is a working day

When we casually say "how many days", we usually mean calendar days — every single day from start to end, weekends included. But for contract deadlines, leave approval, shipping windows and project milestones, what really matters is the working day: the days you can actually work after removing Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. A project may look like 30 calendar days, but after about 8–9 weekend days plus holidays, the real working days are often only around 21 — a one-week gap that can push a delivery past its deadline.

Four real examples (matching the tool's default parameters)

  1. Ordinary month (no holiday): 2026-03-01 to 2026-03-31 is 31 days with 9 weekends, working days = 22 (same whether or not holidays are excluded, since March has no public holiday).
  2. Spanning Qingming: 2026-04-01 to 2026-04-30 is 30 days with 8 weekends; without holiday exclusion you get 22 working days, with exclusion the 1 weekday holiday on Apr 6 (Mon) is removed, giving 21 working days.
  3. Spanning the Labour Day long break: 2026-05-01 to 2026-05-31 is 31 days with 10 weekends; without exclusion 21 working days, with exclusion May 1, May 4 and May 5 (3 weekday holidays) are removed, giving 18 working days — a "31-day" month with only 18 workable days.
  4. National Day golden week: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-08 is 8 days, of which Oct 1–Oct 7 are the National Day holiday; without exclusion you get 6 working days, with exclusion only Oct 8 (Thu) remains, giving 1 working day — almost no effective working days during the long break.

Workday Calculator FAQ

Does the count include both the start and end dates?

Yes, both ends are included — any day that is a working day counts as 1. For example 2026-08-03 (Mon) to 2026-08-07 (Fri) is 5 working days; if you only want the gap between, shift the start or end by one day.

Which 2026 public holidays get deducted?

The tool has New Year (3), Spring Festival (9), Qingming (3), Labour (5), Dragon Boat (3), Mid-Autumn (3) and National Day (7) built in — 33 days total per the State Council notice. Once "Exclude 2026 public holidays" is ticked, the weekday holidays among those 33 days are auto-removed from the working-day count; those on a weekend are already in the weekend exclusion and are not double-counted.

Are make-up workdays (Saturday shifts) counted?

Not automatically. A make-up workday (e.g. Feb 14, May 9, Oct 10 in 2026, all Saturdays) is still treated as a weekend by the tool. The tool currently supports "exclude" but not "add back", so a range crossing such a day will be 1 working day short — check against the official holiday arrangement manually.

How big is the gap between working days and calendar days, and why does it matter?

The gap comes mainly from weekends (about 2/7 of all days) plus holidays. In May 2026, for instance, there are 31 calendar days but only 18 working days — a 13-day difference. Contracts that say "arrives within X working days" or "deliver in 30 working days" will be badly overestimated and underestimate breach risk if you count calendar days — always use the working-day convention for scheduling and approval.

How do I use it for leave or annual-vacation approval?

Five days of annual leave across a weekend actually occupies 7 calendar days but only 5 working days; a contract reading "20 working days from today" — the end date from this tool minus 1 day is the final working day. For half-day precision or sick/personal leave distinctions, fine-tune on top of the result per company policy.

How to read the result

The tool returns the working-day count after removing weekends (and optional holidays), plus three breakdowns: total calendar days, weekend days, and the number of holidays excluded.
