What is Simplified-Traditional Conversion?

Simplified and traditional Chinese share the same language system but differ in the written form of some characters (such as 汉/漢, 见/見). Simplification conversion maps characters between the two forms using a lookup table. This tool includes a common-character table covering high-frequency characters, useful for emails, writing submissions, and cross-border communication.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Paste simplified or traditional Chinese in the "Input text" box
  2. Choose "Simplified → Traditional" or "Traditional → Simplified"
  3. The result updates in real time below
  4. Click "Copy" to copy the converted text

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cover every character? It includes a table of common characters covering high-frequency usage. Rare characters are not guaranteed and are kept unchanged.

Why do some conversions look ambiguous? Some simplified characters map to multiple traditional forms (如 发→發/髮, 后→後/后). This tool uses the most common form; please double-check for special contexts.

Is my data uploaded to a server? No. Everything runs locally in your browser; your input never leaves your device.
