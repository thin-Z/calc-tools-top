About Image Crop Tool

The Image Crop tool is a completely free online tool that lets you crop images by dragging, lock the aspect ratio and download the result. Whether you are a marketer, designer, e-commerce seller or casual user, you can finish the job instantly without installing any software.

Everything is processed locally in your browser and images are never uploaded to a server, so your privacy is protected. The tool supports free ratio plus 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9 presets for avatars, covers and web images.

How to Use Image Crop Tool

  1. Select or drop an image. JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP and GIF are supported.
  2. Drag the crop box to reposition it, drag the corner or edge handles to resize, or drag outside the box to draw a new selection.
  3. Choose an aspect ratio lock (Free / 1:1 / 4:3 / 16:9) as needed.
  4. Click Crop to preview the result, then download as PNG or JPEG.

Image Crop FAQ

Is Image Crop free to use?

Yes, Image Crop is completely free. There are no hidden fees, no account registration and no download required.

Will cropping damage my original image?

No. The original is only processed in memory; you download a new cropped file and your source file is never modified.

Is my data private?

Your privacy is a priority. Everything you enter is processed directly on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Does Image Crop work on mobile?

Yes. The page is fully responsive and supports touch input on phones and tablets as well as desktop browsers.
