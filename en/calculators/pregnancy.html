What is a Pregnancy Calculator?

A pregnancy calculator estimates your due date and current gestational age from your last menstrual period (LMP), helping expectant parents track how far along they are. The due date is typically estimated as LMP + 280 days (about 40 weeks), counting from the first day of your last period.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP)
  2. Optionally set your menstrual cycle length (defaults to 28 days, used only when estimating from a conception date)
  3. Optionally tick "Use conception date" and enter a conception date for a closer estimate
  4. The result panel shows your due date, current gestational age, days pregnant, and days left until due

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the due date exact? No. The due date is an estimate — only about 5% of babies are born on their due date, and most arrive within two weeks either side. Always follow your doctor's assessment and prenatal checkups.

How are days and gestational weeks calculated? Days pregnant is the number of whole days from the first day of your LMP to today; gestational age counts 7 days per week, so weeks = days ÷ 7, plus the remaining days.

Is my data uploaded to a server? No. Everything runs locally in your browser; your input never leaves your device.
