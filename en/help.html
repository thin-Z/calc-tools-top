Help Center & Usage Guide

A guide to using the free online tools on calc-tools.top. All tools run locally in your browser — your data never leaves your device.

How to use this site

Pick a category below, choose a tool, enter your values, and the result updates instantly. Nothing is uploaded; every tool works offline in your browser.

  • Privacy: all processing is local, no upload, no sign-up.
  • Free: all tools are free to use.
  • Bilingual: switch language with the selector in the header.
