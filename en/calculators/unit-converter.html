About the Unit Converter

The Unit Converter is a completely free online tool covering eight categories — length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, data and time — with more than 40 units in total, accurate to four decimal places and computed entirely in your browser. Whether you are a student doing homework, an engineer reading drawings, a shopper comparing overseas listings, or just someone who needs a quick conversion, pick a category, enter a value, choose the "from" and "to" units, and you are done.

How conversion works: route through a base unit

Apart from temperature, all seven other categories use the same logic: convert the source unit to the category's base unit, then convert from the base unit to the target unit. For length the base is the millimeter: 1 meter = 1000 mm and 1 foot = 304.8 mm, so 1 meter = 1000 ÷ 304.8 = 3.2808 feet.

Once you see this, you no longer need to memorise every conversion constant — knowing each unit's factor against the base lets you derive any pair. Temperature is the one exception: Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin have different zero points, so they are not in a pure ratio and must be converted with an offset formula (see below).

Five worked examples

  1. Height: 1.75 m = 5.7415 ft — handy for forms that ask for feet.
  2. Weight: 150 lb = 68.0388 kg — imperial body weight into metric.
  3. Land area: 1 acre = 4,046.86 m² (about 6.07 mu) — useful when reading overseas property listings.
  4. Fuel tank: 50 L = 13.2086 US gallons — North American fuel figures are quoted per gallon.
  5. Speed: 100 km/h = 62.1371 mph — reading speed limits when driving abroad.

Common conversion mistakes

  • Treating temperature as a ratio: 0 °C is not 0 °F. Remove the offset first, then apply the factor — plain multiplication is always wrong.
  • Confusing weight with volume: 1 liter of water weighs about 1 kg, but 1 liter of oil does not — density differs, so the two are not equivalent.
  • Mixing up mu and acre: the Chinese mu (666.67 m²) and the acre (4,046.86 m²) differ by roughly a factor of six.
  • US vs imperial gallon: 3.7854 L against about 4.546 L — about 20% apart, which skews fuel-economy comparisons.
  • 1024 vs 1000 for data: the system and the manufacturer use different bases, which is the real reason a drive looks smaller than advertised.
  • Wrong category: "how big is this plot" is area, not length. Identify what is being measured before converting.

Unit Converter FAQ

How many feet are in a meter?

1 meter = 3.2808 feet. The tool uses the exact definition 1 foot = 0.3048 meters; conversely 1 foot = 0.3048 meters.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32 and °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9, with K = °C + 273.15. Reference points: 0 °C = 32 °F, 37 °C = 98.6 °F, and −40 °C equals −40 °F.

How many square meters are in an acre?

1 acre = 4,046.86 m²; 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.4711 acres. The Chinese mu (亩) is 666.67 m², so 1 acre is about 6.07 mu — different systems, never interchangeable.

How many MB are in a GB?

In binary, 1 GB = 1024 MB and 1 TB = 1024 GB. Drive makers use decimal (1 GB = 1000 MB), so a "1 TB" drive typically shows about 931 GB in your system.

Why can't temperature be converted by simple multiplication?

Length, weight and similar units are pure ratios — one factor is enough. Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin have different zero points (0 °C is 32 °F, not 0 °F), so you must subtract the offset before applying the ratio; multiplying alone gives the wrong answer.
