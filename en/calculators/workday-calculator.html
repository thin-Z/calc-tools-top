Workday Calculator Guide

The Workday Calculator is a completely free, instant online tool that counts working days between two dates. It excludes Saturday and Sunday by default and only counts Monday to Friday. For more precise results, you can exclude 2026 public holidays in one click or manually add custom holiday dates.

Use Cases

Project scheduling and deadline estimation, leave and annual leave approval, contract delivery dates, payroll and attendance checks, event countdown planning — anywhere you need to count by business days.

Counting Rules

The range includes both the start date and the end date. Saturdays and Sundays are not counted. When holiday exclusion is enabled, public holidays that fall on weekdays are also removed. Statutory make-up workdays are not adjusted automatically, so verify against the official holiday schedule.

About Workday Calculator

The Workday Calculator is a completely free online tool that helps you quickly count working days between two dates. Whether you are an HR manager, a project manager, a finance professional or an ordinary employee, you can use it instantly in your browser without installing any software.

Unlike a general date calculator, the Workday Calculator focuses on the days people actually work. It excludes weekends by default and supports year-based public holiday exclusion, making scheduling, approval and attendance checks closer to reality.

How to Use Workday Calculator

  1. Pick a start date and an end date. Today and one week later are pre-filled.
  2. Optionally tick "Exclude 2026 public holidays" or edit the date list manually.
  3. Click Calculate to get the working day count and a full breakdown.
  4. Change any input and the result refreshes automatically.
