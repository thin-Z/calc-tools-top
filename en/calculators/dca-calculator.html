What is DCA (Dollar-Cost Averaging)?

Dollar-cost averaging (DCA / periodic investment) means investing a fixed amount at regular intervals — usually monthly. By spreading your purchases over time it smooths the average cost and reduces the risk of mistiming the market, making it a popular strategy for long-term savings and index/fund investing.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter the amount you plan to invest each month in the "Monthly investment" box
  2. Enter your expected annual return (%) in the "Annual return" box
  3. Enter the number of years in the "Investment period" box — months auto-fill as years × 12, but you can override them
  4. Optionally add an "Initial principal" if you already have a starting amount; the result panel updates in real time

How the Calculation Works

This tool uses the end-of-period annuity model with monthly compounding: future value = initial × (1 + monthly rate)^periods + monthly × [((1 + monthly rate)^periods - 1) / monthly rate], where monthly rate = annual return ÷ 12 ÷ 100. Interest earned = future value - total invested, and total invested = initial + monthly × periods.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the interest compounded?Yes. This calculator compounds monthly, so each month's return is added to the base for the next month, closely matching how fund and DCA accounts accrue.

How do I enter the annual return?Enter your expected long-term average annual return as a percentage (for example, 8 means 8% per year). Past performance is no guarantee of future results — treat the output as an estimate.

Is my data uploaded to a server?No. Everything runs locally in your browser; your input never leaves your device.
