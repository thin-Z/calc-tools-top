About Car Loan Calculator

Car Loan Calculator is a completely free online tool that helps you Car loan monthly payment + interest. Whether you are a casual user or a professional, you can use it instantly without installing any software.

Whether you are planning to buy a car, working at a dealership, or budgeting for a vehicle purchase, you can estimate monthly payments and total interest quickly.

How to Use

  1. Enter the vehicle price in the "Car Price" field.
  2. Enter your down payment amount or percentage.
  3. Enter the loan term (years) and annual interest rate.
  4. Click "Calculate" to see your monthly payment, total interest, and repayment plan.

Car Loan Calculator FAQ

Is Car Loan Calculator free to use?

Yes, Car Loan Calculator is completely free. There are no hidden fees, no account registration and no download required.

How accurate are the results?

All calculations are performed locally in your browser using well-established formulas, so the results are accurate and consistent.

Is my data private?

Your privacy is a priority. Everything you enter is processed directly on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Does Car Loan Calculator work on mobile?

Yes. The page is fully responsive and works smoothly on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

What factors affect my monthly payment?

Your monthly payment depends mainly on the loan amount (price minus down payment), the annual interest rate and the loan term. A longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest paid, while a larger down payment reduces both. Use the sliders to compare scenarios before visiting a dealer.
