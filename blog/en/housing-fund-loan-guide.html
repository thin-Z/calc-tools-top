Housing Fund Loan Guide 2026

A housing provident fund loan (住房公积金贷款) offers eligible employees lower rates than commercial mortgages in many markets. Knowing the rules helps you borrow the maximum at the lowest cost.

Eligibility Basics

Fund loans require a continuous contribution history and an eligible property purchase. Limits on loan size scale with your contribution balance and local caps, so start contributing early to build borrowing capacity.

Rate Advantage

Fund-loan rates are typically set below commercial mortgage rates, which can save significant interest over a 20- or 30-year term. Combining a fund loan with a smaller commercial top-up loan is common when the fund limit falls short.

Combined Loans

When the fund loan cap doesn't cover the purchase, a "fund + commercial" combo loan splits the balance. Compare the blended rate and repayment schedule carefully, because the commercial portion carries the higher cost.

Repayment Tips

Some plans let you raise monthly contributions to speed repayment or withdraw balances for principal reduction. Model both equal-installment and equal-principal options before committing.

Fund Loan vs Commercial Loan Limits

Fund loans usually have lower caps than commercial mortgages, and the maximum scales with your contribution balance and local policy. When the cap is too low, a combined loan covers the gap.

Compare the blended rate and the repayment schedule of a combo loan carefully. The commercial portion costs more, so a larger down payment may be cheaper than borrowing the maximum.

How to Plan a Fund Loan

  1. Check your fund balance and the local maximum loan amount for your situation.
  2. Model the repayment with both equal-installment and equal-principal options.
  3. If the cap falls short, add the commercial top-up portion and compare the blended cost.

Factor in the longer approval time for fund loans when coordinating with the property purchase schedule.
