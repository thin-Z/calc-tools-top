What is a Fraction Calculator?

A fraction calculator performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on two fractions. A fraction consists of a numerator (top) and a denominator (bottom), and the denominator can never be zero. This tool reduces every result to its simplest form (shown as an integer when the denominator is 1) entirely in your browser — your input never leaves your device.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter numbers in the numerator and denominator boxes for the first fraction (denominator not zero; numerators may be negative)
  2. Choose the operation: add (+), subtract (−), multiply (×), or divide (÷)
  3. Enter the numerator and denominator for the second fraction
  4. The result panel shows the full calculation "first fraction [operator] second fraction = result" in real time

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the result simplified? Fractions such as 2/4 and 1/2 represent the same value. This tool reduces the result to its lowest terms, so you can read and use it directly.

Can a denominator be zero? No. A denominator of zero is undefined, and the tool shows "denominator cannot be zero".

What if the second fraction's numerator is zero during division? Dividing by zero is undefined, so the tool shows "divisor cannot be zero".

Is my data uploaded to a server? No. Everything runs locally in your browser; your input never leaves your device.
